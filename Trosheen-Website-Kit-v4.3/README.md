# Trosheen Website Kit v4.3: "The house before the shop"

v4.3 (16 Oct 2026): more motion. 18 new Lotties (55 in total): living making steps, category cards, care, story, contacts, newsletter and a page loader. Plus a soft page-to-page veil, a breathing hero photo, count-up years, Gilt marker words, magnetic buttons and photo hover zoom. No generated illustrations anywhere: every piece is shown in your own photo (13-code/demo-assets/piece-01 … 16). Motion only plays in a browser: unzip first, then open 13-code/pages/index.html.

Everything needed to rebuild trosheen.shop: a fresh design and construction book, assets made from your own photos, motion, and working code.

## Start here
1. **index.html**: visual overview of the kit (open it in a browser).
2. **01-design-construction-book/**: the book (36 pages). Designers read sections 03–12; developers start at section 13.
3. **13-code/pages/index.html**: the working prototype. All 8 pages are linked: Sākums, Veikals (with the Pielāgota darbnīca tab), Mūsu stāsts, Galerija, Žurnāls, Kopšana, Kontakti, plus a product page.

## Rules that never bend
- No text, logos or buttons inside images. Headlines and buttons are real HTML.
- Real pieces only: never recolour, replace or invent.
- Only the confirmed round T Maker's Seal family.
- Gallery, Žurnāls and Veikals stay in the nav even when empty, with warm "coming soon" states.
- Latvian copy needs a fluent speaker's approval before launch.
